# Image attribution

Most historical source portraits added to this directory are public-domain or CC0 works obtained through Wikimedia Commons. Their corresponding cartoon variants were generated from those source portraits and then cropped, resized, and given transparent backgrounds for Paper Trails.

Public-domain source portraits added for the 1400–1600 expansion:

- `regiomontanus.jpg` is a cropped and resized version of a portrait scan attributed on Wikimedia Commons to the Smithsonian Libraries; the file is marked public domain. [Source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Johannes_Regiomontanus.jpg). `cartoons/regiomontanus.png` is an AI-assisted illustrated adaptation.
- `copernicus.jpg` is a cropped and resized version of the circa-1580 Toruń Town Hall portrait of Nicolaus Copernicus; the file is marked public domain. [Source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Nikolaus_Kopernikus_MOT.jpg). `cartoons/copernicus.png` is an AI-assisted illustrated adaptation.
- `vesalius.jpg` is a cropped and resized version of a public-domain portrait of Andreas Vesalius engraved by Cornelis van Dalen II after a design associated with Titian. [Source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Andreas_Vesalius,_PA00128.jpg). `cartoons/vesalius.png` is an AI-assisted illustrated adaptation.
- `brahe_tycho.jpg` is a cropped and resized version of a sixteenth-century portrait of Tycho Brahe from Frederiksborg Museum; the file is marked public domain. [Source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Tycho_Brahe_2.jpg). `cartoons/brahe_tycho.png` is an AI-assisted illustrated adaptation.

The following files retain Creative Commons terms from their source works:

- `cavendish_henry.jpg` is a cropped and resized version of *Henry Cavendish. Aquatint by C. Rosenberg after W. Alexander*, supplied by the Wellcome Collection and licensed [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). [Source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Henry_Cavendish._Aquatint_by_C._Rosenberg_after_W._Alexander_Wellcome_V0001039.jpg). `cartoons/cavendish_henry.png` is an AI-assisted illustrated adaptation of that image.
- `green_george.jpg` is a cropped copy of the George Green Medal photograph by Alexhamid, licensed [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). [Source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:GeorgeGreenMedal_72dpi_250px.jpg). It is retained as source history but is not used as a likeness.
- `brans_carl.jpg` is a cropped and resized version of the Carl Brans photograph by Cfbolner, licensed [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). [Source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Carl-brans-loyola-university-new-orleans.jpg). `cartoons/brans_carl.png` is an AI-assisted illustrated adaptation and is also licensed CC BY-SA 4.0.
- `wheeler.jpg` is a cropped and resized version of the John Archibald Wheeler portrait from the AIP Emilio Segrè Visual Archives. The American Institute of Physics permits reuse and derivative works with attribution. [Source and licence](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:John_Archibald_Wheeler_signed.jpg). `cartoons/wheeler.png` is an AI-assisted illustrated adaptation subject to the same attribution requirement.
- `breit.jpg` is a cropped version of the Gregory Breit portrait supplied through the National Academy of Sciences and Wikimedia Commons. The National Academy of Sciences permits reuse and derivative works with attribution. [Source and licence](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Gregory_Breit.jpg). `cartoons/breit.png` is an AI-assisted illustrated adaptation subject to the same attribution requirement.

Robert Hooke, George Green, Ewald Georg von Kleist, John Michell, and Steve K. Lamoreaux currently use the project's neutral `default.png` photograph and `cartoons/default.png` illustrated question mark. The shared cartoon avoids invented or faceless human likenesses when the configured data has no trustworthy portrait. `cartoons/default.png` is an AI-assisted original generated for Paper Trails in The Wry Engraver house style.
