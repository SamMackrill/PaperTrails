# Conference photo attribution

## Rights policy

Conference photographs are third-party works and are not covered by this project's Unlicense. For this project's United States rights assessment, any photograph first published anywhere in the world before January 1, 1931, is treated as public domain in the United States. This cutoff does not apply merely because a photograph was *created* before 1931, does not resolve the status of an unpublished work, and does not state its copyright status outside the United States. The U.S. Copyright Office explains that restored foreign works published before 1978 generally receive a 95-year U.S. term in [Circular 38B](https://www.copyright.gov/circs/circ38b.pdf).

Where a photograph is included on a U.S. fair-use basis, that is a case-specific legal rationale rather than a licence. The U.S. Copyright Office notes that only a court can conclusively decide fair use and identifies the four factors in its [fair-use guidance](https://www.copyright.gov/fair-use/more-info.html). Such files must not be reused under the repository's Unlicense without an independent legal basis.
