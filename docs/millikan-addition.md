# Robert Andrews Millikan

The discovery record already described Millikan's 1913 oil-drop result but had no scientist profile or publication links. The new `millikan` profile supplies a photographic portrait, a transparent Wry Engraver illustration, their 192px WebP timeline thumbnails, two original papers, and the discovery's bidirectional scientist link. The timeline anchor is the 1913 paper; the existing historical discovery date and charge trail are preserved.

## Verified sources

- [Nobel biography](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/physics/1923/millikan/biographical/) and [National Academy of Sciences memoir](https://www.nasonline.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/millikan-robert.pdf): identity, life dates, American nationality, and academic appointments. The affiliation list covers Oberlin, Columbia, Chicago and Caltech; it does not attempt to list every visit or honorary association.
- [Original 1913 journal record](https://journals.aps.org/pr/abstract/10.1103/PhysRev.2.109): *On the Elementary Electrical Charge and the Avogadro Constant*, Physical Review 2, 109–143.
- [Original 1916 journal record](https://journals.aps.org/pr/abstract/10.1103/PhysRev.7.355): *A Direct Photoelectric Determination of Planck's "h"*, Physical Review 7, 355–388.
- [Photograph provenance and U.S. rights](https://library.si.edu/image-gallery/73673): Smithsonian Libraries and Archives, *Portrait of Robert Andrews Millikan*, identifier `DAMS:SIL-SIL14-m004-03`, explicitly marked **No Copyright – United States**. The [institutional reuse explanation](https://library.si.edu/copyright) explains this rights statement. Courtesy of the Smithsonian Libraries and Archives. Photographer and historical photograph date are not supplied. The original 1274×2000 JPEG was resized without stretching and padded on white to 640×640. This replaces the former Nobel Foundation photograph, whose cited page supplied only a Swedish rights rationale.

## Illustration

Built-in image generation used the verified Smithsonian photograph as the identity reference and `rutherford.png`, `bohr.png`, and `alfven.png` as style references. The former illustration was replaced rather than retained as an adaptation of the superseded photograph. The new source's characteristic swept silver hair, strong brow and spotted bow tie guide its likeness.

The original generated PNG is retained outside the repository. Its 1254×1254 output was normalized with the repository's approved deterministic finisher, which removed zero background pixels. The final `images/cartoons/millikan.png` passes the alpha and 1024×1024 validation. Three-way source/before/after review, dark/light proof, and 92px colour / 42px grayscale circular crops were inspected. Both WebP thumbnails were regenerated from the replacement masters.

The [complete generation and preparation record](millikan-portrait-provenance.json) contains the exact prompt, source catalogue and original-download URLs, institutional rights statement, reference files and asset hashes. This records the Smithsonian's U.S. statement without inferring an unknown photographer, historical date or worldwide rights waiver.
