# Camera photography and colour film

Two requested milestones are represented in the scientific milestone lane in all modes, and by two new complete top-border ribbons in Tapestry. The source record supplies English details and links when either camera motif is selected. The standing border brief is updated in [context-drawing-instructions.html](context-drawing-instructions.html).

- **1827 — First Surviving Camera Photograph.** The [Harry Ransom Center](https://www.hrc.utexas.edu/niepce-heliograph/), which holds Niépce's *View from the Window at Le Gras*, dates it to 1827. Older accounts give 1826. This is a permanent camera-photography milestone, rather than an invention date for the centuries-old camera obscura. The embroidery shows a rigid wooden camera, short brass lens, pewter plate, schematic courtyard image and bitumen-preparation equipment.
- **1935 — Kodachrome Colour Film Introduced.** [Kodak's chronology](https://www.kodak.com/en/company/page/milestones/) records its launch in 16 mm movie-film format; 35 mm slides and 8 mm followed in 1936. The [George Eastman Museum / FIAF Film Atlas](https://www.filmatlas.com/entry/544) credits Mannes and Godowsky and explains the three-colour process. Earlier colour photography and film systems existed. The embroidery uses a compact spring-wound cine camera, detached reel, perforated film frames, developing glassware and symbolic colour layers. An initial studio-camera-like depiction was corrected before integration.

## Artwork and geometry

Built-in `image_gen` created the two new transparent PNG masters. Their bytes are retained as generated, with WebP runtime derivatives. Exact initial and correction prompts, identity of every supplied style/edit reference, and file hashes are recorded in [photography-border-generation.json](../images/tapestry/photography-border-generation.json).

The new complete strips replace `nineteenth-1` and `electrons-5`; all existing atlas files and the other 94 border ribbons remain unchanged. Measured crop rectangles preserve native proportions. Both chronological border streams still use one constant depth within the shared context area, with no repeated or stretched filler and one continuous linen base. The same fixed pixels and their selection targets are concealed and revealed by the cloth's folds.

Editorial camera anchors at 1827 and 1935 centre the view on the corresponding drawn camera. These anchors locate symbolic artwork; canonical milestone years remain in the data and are not inferred from picture widths.

## Validation

The border provenance audit checks both new masters, their derivatives, prompts, supplied references and measured source pixels. Tests check chronology, stable legacy aliases, centred dated anchors at multiple context sizes, and hit areas clipped to visible source pixels throughout folding. Local browser review also checks both milestone panels, source links and selection from the actual border artwork.
