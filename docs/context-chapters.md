## Composition and dates

Four sourced context records add nine editorial chapters: British industrialisation
(c. 1760–1830), transatlantic enslavement and resistance (1501–1866 estimates
window), the Haitian Revolution (1791–1804), and electric telegraph networks
(c. 1830–1866). Scope, dating qualifications, chapter descriptions and evidence
appear in the record panel. SlaveVoyages coverage is explicitly distinguished
from the beginning or end of slavery. The original 21 IDs and legacy positions
are unchanged; new legacy positions are appended.

`clothComposition.js` plans 30 event/chapter pictures, each used once. Source
images retain a uniform scale and are clipped to their dated chapter footprints;
unused stretches remain quiet cloth. Chapter anchors use the same linear or
density mapping as the scientific timeline. Chronological painter precedence
matches the existing caption and event selection order. Broad periods can overlap
visually; their individual braids and record panels remain independent.

Six distinct neutral panoramas appear once each, over continuous deterministic
thread paths and individually varied vegetation. Nothing is tiled or mirrored.
Normal textures feather joins inside picture boundaries. SVG fallback uses narrow
graduated linen bands instead of masks, which proved unreliable inside retained
`use` surfaces. Missing required event artwork prevents installing an incomplete
texture; the original-art fallback for pre-existing events remains available.

The preceding fixed-art box-pleat model is preserved: flat overview fronts stay
visible, details are concealed and exposed rather than squeezed, and all folds
flatten at maximum zoom. No material or image swaps occur during zoom reversal.
Generated atlas crops omit the later railway-heavy portion of the industrial
community illustration. Full original prompts and provenance for both new PNGs
are in `images/tapestry/README.md`.
