# Stern–Gerlach scientist additions

The existing 1922 discovery names both experimenters but has no linked profiles. This layer adds `stern` and `gerlach`, their photographs, matching transparent Wry Engraver illustrations, 192px WebP thumbnails, four publication records and the discovery's two-way profile links. The joint directional-quantization paper appears under each author, with distinct stable publication IDs.

## Biographical evidence

- [Stern's Nobel facts](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/physics/1943/stern/facts/) and [Nobel biography](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/physics/1943/stern/biographical/): 17 February 1888–17 August 1969; Breslau doctorate, ETH Zurich, Frankfurt, Rostock, Hamburg and Carnegie appointments. [American National Biography](https://www.anb.org/view/10.1093/anb/9780198606697.001.0001/anb-9780198606697-e-1301581) records naturalization in 1939, supporting German-American nationality. [Lindau research profile](https://www.mediatheque.lindau-nobel.org/research-profile/laureate-stern) corroborates his experimental contributions and education.
- [Gerlach's scholarly biography](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-030-63963-1_8), by Josef Georg Huber, Horst Schmidt-Böcking and Bretislav Friedrich: 1 August 1889–10 August 1979; Tübingen doctorate and professorship, Frankfurt experimental work, Munich professorship and rectorate, and Bonn institute directorship. [Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Walther-Gerlach) independently corroborates dates and German nationality. The selected university affiliations cover the principal posts rather than every visit; Göttingen teaching is verified but omitted from this concise list.

## Original publications

- Gerlach and Stern, 1922, [Der experimentelle Nachweis des magnetischen Moments des Silberatoms](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/BF01329580), DOI `10.1007/BF01329580`. Listed under Gerlach.
- Gerlach and Stern, 1922, [Der experimentelle Nachweis der Richtungsquantelung im Magnetfeld](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/BF01326983), DOI `10.1007/BF01326983`. Listed under both authors.
- Frisch and Stern, 1933, [Über die magnetische Ablenkung von Wasserstoffmolekülen und das magnetische Moment des Protons. I](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/BF01330773), DOI `10.1007/BF01330773`. Listed under Stern.

Both profiles anchor at their selected 1922 works. The discovery's existing date and explanation are preserved: the experiment demonstrated spatial quantization, while the electron-spin interpretation followed later.

## Portrait evidence and preparation

- [AIP Stern catalogue](https://repository.aip.org/node/64141), **Stern Otto F2**, **PH 1998-2931**. Credit: **AIP Emilio Segrè Visual Archives, Segrè Collection**.
- [AIP Gerlach catalogue](https://repository.aip.org/node/59036), **Gerlach Walther B1**, approximately 1925. Credit: **AIP Emilio Segrè Visual Archives, Lande Collection**.

Both catalogues explicitly identify **American Institute of Physics (AIP)** as copyright holder. [AIP's copyright policy](https://aip.libguides.com/esvaguide/copyright) freely grants use of AIP-owned images with their listed credits. Stern's [Commons copy](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Otto_Stern_smoking_a_cigar_while_in_laboratory.jpg) also records CC BY 4.0. No Creative Commons or public-domain status is inferred for Gerlach; AIP's direct permission is retained alongside the assets, illustrated adaptations and thumbnails.

Original JPEGs were downloaded at 3489×2376 and 3953×2424, then cropped to head-and-shoulders squares and resized to 640×640. Gerlach's shadow is excluded from the identity crop. Unknown photographers and Stern's unknown photograph date remain unknown; catalogue ingestion dates are not treated as historical photograph dates.

Each illustration was generated separately with the built-in tool, its corresponding photograph as the sole identity reference and Rutherford, Bohr and Alfvén as style references. The original 1254×1254 outputs had real alpha; the approved deterministic finisher normalized them to 1024×1024 and removed zero background pixels. Both pass the portrait validator. Three-way source/fallback/new comparisons, dark/light backgrounds and 92px colour / 42px grayscale circular crops were inspected.

The [generation and preparation record](stern-gerlach-portrait-provenance.json) contains exact prompts, source catalogue URLs, original download URLs, crop coordinates, rights statements, reference hashes and final asset hashes. Sources with restricted or unclear underlying rights were not used.

Existing verified institutional assets are reused for ETH Zurich, Hamburg, Tübingen, Munich and Bonn. Breslau, Frankfurt, Rostock and Carnegie use neutral icons; README's exact neutral-affiliation count includes both Frankfurt rows.

## Validation

All 123 native tests pass, as does the complete browser audit covering existing context views, photography border selection, relations, scientist lifespans, trails and printing. Collaborative-browser inspection confirmed both new profile panels, photo and cartoon thumbnails, all four publication panels and their DOI/source links, and discovery-to-profile / profile-to-discovery navigation. Both themes were exercised. Parsed totals are 129 scientists, 243 publication records and 22 affiliation rows without emblems; new colours and record identities are unique.
