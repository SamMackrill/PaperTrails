# Aharonov, Aspect and Dalibard

Three scientists named in existing discovery records now have complete profiles, two original publication records each, photo/illustration masters and 192px WebP thumbnails. The Aharonov–Bohm record links `aharonov` and `bohm`; the changing-analyzer Bell-test record links `aspect` and `dalibard`, retaining Bell as theorist. Gérard Roger's name and historical contribution remain in the original record; his profile needs further archival research rather than guessed biographical details.

## Identity, education and appointments

- **Yakir Aharonov**, born **28 August 1932**, Israeli. [Chapman University](https://www.chapman.edu/our-faculty/yakir-aharonov.aspx) supplies the full birth date, Technion undergraduate degree, Bristol doctorate and current Chapman professorship / Institute for Quantum Studies directorship, with Tel Aviv emeritus status. The [Wolf Foundation](https://wolffund.org.il/yakir-aharonov/) corroborates the degrees and 1932 birth year. The [English Wikipedia identity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yakir_Aharonov) was checked against the faculty profile. Affiliations cover Technion, Bristol, Tel Aviv and Chapman rather than every teaching post.
- **Alain Aspect**, born **15 June 1947**, French. [Nobel facts](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/physics/2022/aspect/facts/) supplies the full birth date and Institut d'Optique / École Polytechnique affiliations. [École Polytechnique's institutional biography](https://www.polytechnique.edu/en/press-room/press-releases/2022-nobel-prize-physics-has-been-awarded-alain-aspect-his-research-quantum-physics) corroborates his education at ENSET / ENS Cachan and work at Collège de France, École Polytechnique and Institut d'Optique. Cachan is a separate institution from the Paris ENS; the latter's emblem is not substituted. [English Wikipedia identity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alain_Aspect) is supplied for further reading.
- **Jean Dalibard**, born **8 December 1958**, French. [ENS's profile](https://www.ens.psl.eu/actualites/jean-dalibard-0) supplies the full birth date, education and appointments. [Collège de France's biography](https://www.college-de-france.fr/en/chair/jean-dalibard-atoms-and-radiation-statutory-chair/biography) corroborates ENS studies, research in the Kastler Brossel Laboratory, the 1989–2015 École Polytechnique professorship and his Atoms and Radiation chair. [Optica](https://www.optica.org/history/biographies/bios/Jean_Dalibard) corroborates his contributions to atom-light interactions, laser cooling and quantum gases. [English Wikipedia identity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean_Dalibard) was checked for name, full date and French nationality.

The three are living scientists in these current institutional records and have empty death values. No precise historical dates are guessed. Bristol, Paris ENS, École Polytechnique and Collège de France reuse the existing verified assets. Five new affiliation rows without verified emblems use neutral icons, bringing the exact total to 27.

## Portrait rights and drawing

- **Aharonov:** Kobi Kalmanovitz's photograph supplied by the EMET Prize website, [Commons file](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Yakir_aharonov.jpg), **CC BY-SA 3.0**, copyright-holder permission recorded as VRT **2014120210018751**. The 540×315 source has a 210px square identity crop, modestly resized to 256px. No invented photo detail or extreme upscaling. Date metadata is retained as supplied rather than presented as independently verified day-level historical evidence.
- **Aspect:** Royal Society's [2015 portrait](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Alain-Aspect-ForMemRS.jpg), uploaded by Royal Society uploader; **CC BY-SA 4.0**, chosen from its dual 3.0/4.0 grant, with VRT **2014062710019796**. Original 3724×5586 file; 3000px square crop, reduced to 640px. No separately unnamed photographer is invented.
- **Dalibard:** **Jérémy Barande / École polytechnique**, [Commons crop](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Jean_Dalibard_(cropped).jpg) and [institutional Flickr source](https://www.flickr.com/photos/117994717@N06/14002744812), **CC BY-SA 2.0**, credit **© Barande Jérémy / EP.** Original crop 1069×1430; square recrop reduced to 640px.

Each illustrated adaptation and both corresponding WebP thumbnails retain the source's chosen share-alike licence and credit, as recorded in [images/ATTRIBUTION.md](../images/ATTRIBUTION.md). These files are exceptions to the project's Unlicense.

Each new illustration used its scientist's own photograph as the sole identity reference, with Rutherford, Bohr and Alfvén supplying only the graphic house style. Aspect received one focused correction to simplify the skin drawing finish. All outputs were generated through the built-in tool, normalized by the approved deterministic finisher and checked for real alpha. No substantive bitmap drawing was performed with Python. The [provenance record](quantum-scientist-portrait-provenance.json) records exact prompts, source permissions, reference hashes, crop coordinates, initial/corrected Aspect generation chain and final asset hashes.

Three-way comparisons and dark/light backgrounds were inspected. Dalibard's validator warning reflects the profile silhouette and empty space beside the spectacles, not holes in the face. Final acceptance includes 92px colour and 42px grayscale circular crops.
