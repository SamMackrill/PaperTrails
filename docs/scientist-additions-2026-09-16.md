## Date, interpretation and affiliation decisions

- Richer's birth is approximate (around 1630); both life dates remain year-only. No verified university association or reliable reusable likeness was found, so he uses the existing neutral photograph and illustrated question mark.
- Bradley's birth year is disputed. The entry follows the Oxford DNB's 1692 rather than inventing a day; the publication record dates the aberration letter to 1728, while its Royal Society reading is often described as January 1729.
- Halley's dates are Gregorian. His death on 14 January 1742 Old Style corresponds to 25 January Gregorian.
- Herschel's 1781 title calls Uranus a comet because that was his initial interpretation. No honorary degree is used as an academic affiliation.
- Leavitt's 1912 circular was issued by Pickering and explicitly credits her work. Radcliffe's historical predecessor is identified in the affiliation.
- Bell's birthday is 28 July, not the June date repeated by some secondary sources.
- Steinmetz's Union teaching is well documented. Conflicting accounts of an earned doctorate are not incorporated.
- Miller's non-null observation is recorded as a claim, with its later reassessment. Dingle's book is documented as an objection to relativity, not as an established disproof.
- Existing verified institutional assets are reused. Ten added affiliation rows omit `coat`; README records these neutral icons.

## Portrait preparation and review

Fifteen individual illustrated portraits use the source photographs/paintings as identity references, with `maxwell.png` and `bohr.png` as house-style references. Richer retains the shared neutral question mark. Dingle, Tomonaga and Lodge's narrow source photographs have neutral side margins to preserve the full face in circular crops; original image detail is not upscaled.

Generation uses the Wry Engraver prompt: a newly drawn, gently caricatured head-and-shoulders bust, bold dark blue-green contours, muted flat colour, restrained natural skin, a small amused or inquisitive expression, no text or props, and safe margins for 92 px and 42 px circles. Every prompt explicitly requests genuine alpha transparency and forbids a depicted checkerboard, backdrop, halo or background colour.

Raw outputs are staged outside the repository and decoded with the portrait validator. Accepted candidates are normalized to 1024×1024 RGBA. The Cassini and Halley candidates required removal of an inspected pale neutral baked checkerboard with `-BackgroundMinimum 185`; the finisher's default remains 210. A dark-checkerboard Bradley attempt was rejected and regenerated. Finished images are checked against the source and shared default on light/dark backgrounds and in circular crops before promotion.

The [final portrait proof sheet](scientist-additions-2026-09-16-portraits.png) shows all sixteen additions at 92 px in colour and 42 px in grayscale, including Richer's shared neutral portrait.
