# Historical scientists named in discovery records

William Derham, Samuel Molyneux and Christopher Clavius now have profiles, two verified publication records each and links to the existing speed-of-sound, stellar-aberration and Gregorian-calendar discoveries. Existing discovery dates, descriptions and credit remain unchanged. Lilius's separate contribution remains visible in the calendar credit and will receive its own profile in the following discovery-anchored layer.

## Identity and institutions

- **William Derham**, English, **26 November 1657–5 April 1735**. [Brycchan Carey's British Academy/Wolfson-funded naturalist catalogue](https://brycchancarey.com/naturalists/william_derham.html) supplies dates and Trinity College, Oxford; [Oxford Reference](https://www.oxfordreference.com/display/10.1093/oi/authority.20110803095711918) corroborates both dates and the Oxford BA. [English Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Derham) identifies the same natural philosopher. The affiliation uses a neutral icon; Cambridge and Dublin Trinity assets would represent different colleges.
- **Samuel Molyneux**, British, **18 July 1689–13 April 1728**. [Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Samuel-Molyneux) and [University of St Andrews](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/Biographies/Molyneux_Samuel/) agree on these exact dates and Trinity College Dublin's BA/MA. Britannica supplies the compact nationality descriptor. [English Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samuel_Molyneux) and [Linda Hall Library](https://www.lindahall.org/about/news/scientist-of-the-day/samuel-molyneux/) instead give 16 July; the selected day follows the two agreeing institutional references. The impossible 1685 Samuel/Huygens meeting described in St Andrews is not repeated or used: Samuel was not yet born. Verified joint observations do not make Molyneux the author of Bradley's aberration paper.
- **Christopher Clavius**, German, **25 March 1538–6 February 1612**, following [Society of Catholic Scientists](https://catholicscientists.org/scientists-of-the-past/christopher-clavius/). [St Andrews](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/Biographies/Clavius/) corroborates the birth date but lists 2 February for death; [NCAR/UCAR](https://www2.hao.ucar.edu/education/scientists/christoph-clavius-1537-1612) corroborates 6 February but gives 1537 as the birth year, also used by the BSB authority record. These historical source disagreements remain explicit here rather than silently claiming unanimity. Coimbra studies and the Collegio Romano mathematics professorship are supported by NCAR/St Andrews. Both use neutral icons; no successor-institution emblem is substituted.

These three affiliations bring the exact neutral-icon row count to **33**. Molyneux reuses the existing, attributed Trinity College Dublin asset.

## Portraits and rights

- Derham: anonymous eighteenth-century engraving, scanned from an eighteenth-century text; [Commons source and rights](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:William_Derham.jpg). The underlying published engraving predates U.S. copyright cutoffs; Commons records public-domain art and a faithful reproduction. **11 March 2012 is the scan/upload metadata, not the engraving date.** No artist or historical day is invented.
- Clavius: **Francesco Villamena, 1606**, Rijksmuseum **RP-P-OB-38.439**, [institutional record](http://hdl.handle.net/10934/RM0001.COLLECT.188648), [Commons crop and CC0 1.0 rights](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Portret_van_astronoom_Christoph_Clavius,_RP-P-OB-38.439_(cropped).jpg).
- Molyneux: no verified historical likeness was found in this audit; Linda Hall explicitly reports the same difficulty. His profile uses the existing shared photographic and illustrated defaults. His father William, his uncle Thomas and Samuel Molyneux Madden are not substituted, and no invented face is generated.

The two source engravings are cropped to true RGB JPEGs; the drawings are fresh built-in image generations using only their own likeness for identity plus three approved house-style references. Exact prompts, source and final hashes, crops and rights are in [the provenance record](historical-scientist-portrait-provenance.json). Both cartoons are validated 1024px RGBA PNGs, with photo and cartoon WebP thumbnails. Original downloads and review sheets remain outside the repository.
