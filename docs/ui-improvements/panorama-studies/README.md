# Panorama composition studies

Requested during Phase 3 of the second-pass UI review, 29 September 2026.

The current artwork style is liked. These limited studies compare composition and joins while retaining the embroidery character. They do not replace the app’s assets or validate historical depictions. All dates must remain in the HTML interface rather than generated art. Locations and subject sizes in these studies are not mapped to time.

Selected direction: **B — Continuous landscape**, confirmed in Phase 5. A and C are retained as comparison studies.

Generation method: built-in image generation tool. Style source: `../../../images/tapestry/revolutions-panorama-v2.png`. All three generated PNG files are preserved without pixel modification and embedded in the standalone report.

## Study A — Joined vignettes

Output: `study-a-joined-vignettes.png`.

```text
Use case: historical-scene. Asset type: limited composition study for Paper Trails, a scientific-history timeline. Reinterpret the supplied local seven-row tapestry atlas into ONE NEW SINGLE HORIZONTAL PANORAMA, 3:1 landscape aspect ratio, with only THREE connected symbolic subjects in left-to-right chronological order: War of the Spanish Succession (early eighteenth-century soldiers, courier and disputed crown motif); Great Frost of 1709 (bare frozen trees, icebound village and people gathering firewood); Seven Years' War (eighteenth-century officers with a map, artillery and a distant ship). Input image is the style source, not a layout to reproduce. Preserve its charming flat side-on embroidered figures, dark stitched outlines, subtle woven warm unbleached linen, muted indigo, madder, sage and ochre. Historically appropriate early/mid-eighteenth-century clothing. Keep all important figures the same visual height and keep one shared ground/horizon throughout. These are symbolic tableaux, not documentary reconstructions or a claim that the events are causally linked. All three subjects must be visibly distinct while transitions are coherent. No text, letters, numerals, dates, labels, watermark, explanatory arrows, UI, rectangular panels, hard vertical edges, photorealism or gore. Output one finished panorama, not an atlas, grid or multiple alternatives.
Variant A — Joined vignettes, closest to the current artwork. Compose three compact narrative clusters separated by short connecting passages of embroidered plants and low countryside. Link them with one consistent ground baseline and tiny repeated stitch motifs. Keep the flat vignette character; bring neighbouring figures, terrain and architectural scale into agreement. Show warm village ground gradually cooling into frost then returning to earth tones. Use soft transitions with substantial linen showing, not hard scene cuts.
```
