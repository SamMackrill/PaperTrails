# Experimental scientists completing the missing-profile audit

This layer adds Robert Curtis Retherford and Gérard Roger, the last two candidates in the discovery/conference scientist audit. Both have verified coauthored original papers, so their earliest selected publication remains the timeline anchor: **1947** and **1982**. The Lamb-shift discovery now links Lamb and Retherford; the changing-analyzer Bell test links Aspect, Dalibard and Roger while retaining Bell's separate theoretical credit. Existing discovery dates, descriptions and persistent identities are preserved.

## Biographical evidence and explicit limits

- **Robert Curtis Retherford**, American, **1912–1981**. [Treccani](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/robert-curtis-retherford/) identifies the same experimental physicist, birthplace and life years; [Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Robert-Curtis-Retherford) identifies him as American. [English Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Retherford) corroborates the life years and identity, but is not the sole authority. No precise day/month or exact age is invented. The original 1947 APS byline gives the Columbia Radiation Laboratory affiliation; the 1950 paper explicitly states that Retherford submitted the work towards his Columbia doctorate. Its publication date does not establish a separate doctorate-conferral date. A later Chicago appointment mentioned by Treccani is not added without corroboration.
- **Gérard Roger** is explicitly identified as an engineer in Aspect's experimental team by the [Académie française's account](https://www.academie-francaise.fr/reponse-au-discours-de-reception-de-m-alain-aspect). [CNRS's history](https://news.cnrs.fr/articles/alain-aspect-shedding-new-light-on-light-and-atoms) independently names him among the Orsay Bell-test experimenters. Original APS bylines establish the Institut d'Optique affiliation. Its existing Graduate School institution name is reused with a neutral icon, bringing the exact neutral-affiliation count to **35**. No reliable individual biography establishes Roger's birth, death or nationality; those fields are empty, without implying that he is alive or assigning French citizenship from his workplace. No individual English Wikipedia article was verified, so institutional accounts replace a guessed Wikipedia URL. With no birth year, no lifespan or "living" label is drawn; his verified paper dates still position the profile and connectors correctly.
