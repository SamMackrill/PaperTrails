# Christian Doppler and Woldemar Voigt additions

## Christian Doppler (`doppler`)

Profile focus: wave-frequency shift from relative motion between source and observer (the Doppler effect). Nationality: Austrian. Life dates: `1803-11-29` to `1853-03-17`. The earliest listed work anchors the timeline portrait at `1842`.

### Biography and academic associations

- [Österreichisches Biographisches Lexikon (ÖBL)](https://www.biographien.ac.at/oebl/oebl_D/Doppler_Christian_1803_1853.xml) confirms 29 November 1803 (Salzburg) to 17 March 1853 (Venice) and chronicles his studies and teaching at the Vienna Polytechnic Institute, Prague Polytechnic Institute, Mining and Forestry Academy in Schemnitz, and the University of Vienna (first director of the Institute of Physics from 1850).
- [MacTutor History of Mathematics](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/Biographies/Doppler/) corroborates his Austrian nationality, life dates, studies in Vienna, professorships in Prague and Schemnitz, and return to Vienna.
- [University of Vienna *650 plus*](https://geschichte.univie.ac.at/en/persons/christian-doppler) corroborates his life dates and appointment as professor of experimental physics and first director of the Physics Institute at the University of Vienna.
- [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian_Doppler) is included in `links` as the standard Wikipedia reference.
- University of Vienna reuses `images/institutions/university-of-vienna.png`. Vienna Polytechnic Institute, Prague Polytechnic Institute, and Mining and Forestry Academy, Schemnitz have no verified local emblems and use the neutral icon; University of Vienna and Charles University emblems are not substituted for the polytechnic institutes. README's exact neutral-affiliation count increases from 36 to 39.

## Woldemar Voigt (`voigt`)

Profile focus: crystal physics, tensor terminology and Voigt notation, the magneto-optical Voigt effect, the Voigt transformation, and the Voigt spectral line profile. Nationality: German. Life dates: `1850-09-02` to `1919-12-13`. The earliest listed work anchors the timeline portrait at `1887`.

### Biography and academic associations

- [Deutsche Biographie (GND 117478008)](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/gnd117478008.html) confirms 1850–1919, birth in Leipzig, death in Göttingen, and his career as physicist and university professor.
- [Treccani Enciclopedia](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/woldemar-voigt/) corroborates his Leipzig birth, Königsberg professorship (from 1875), Göttingen chair (from 1883), and crystal-physics monograph *Lehrbuch der Kristallphysik* (1910).
- Berlin-Brandenburgische Akademie der Wissenschaften membership records confirm the exact life dates 2 September 1850 (Leipzig) and 13 December 1919 (Göttingen).
- [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woldemar_Voigt) is included in `links` as the standard Wikipedia reference.
- All three academic affiliations (Leipzig University, University of Königsberg, University of Göttingen) reuse existing verified local emblems.

### Selected original works

1. **1887 — *Ueber das Doppler'sche Princip*.** *Nachrichten von der Königlichen Gesellschaft der Wissenschaften und der Georg-Augusts-Universität zu Göttingen*, No. 2 (1887), pp. 41–51, [Göttinger Digitalisierungszentrum (GDZ)](https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/de/id/PPN252457072_1887).
2. **1910 — *Lehrbuch der Kristallphysik (mit Ausschluss der Kristalloptik)*.** Leipzig and Berlin: B. G. Teubner (1910), [Internet Archive](https://archive.org/details/bub_gb_SvPPAAAAMAAJ).
3. **1912 — *Über das Gesetz der Intensitätsverteilung innerhalb der Linien eines Gasspektrums*.** *Sitzungsberichte der Königlich Bayerischen Akademie der Wissenschaften zu München* (1912, 25), pp. 603–620, [Bayerische Akademie der Wissenschaften](https://publikationen.badw.de/de/003395768).
